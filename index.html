Frequently asked questions

One gateway, the right local destination

What happens when a country has its own portal?

You are taken directly to that country's CasaStudent website, with local language, cities and listings.

What happens for all other countries?

The country opens inside CasaStudent.eu and works like a city page, with its main university destinations.

What kind of housing is CasaStudent for?

Student housing for semesters, academic years and other medium- or long-term stays.
